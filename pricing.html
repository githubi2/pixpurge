Still Have Questions?

Find answers to the most common questions about credits, subscriptions, billing, and refunds.

What are credits and how do they work?
Credits are used to process images and to read text from them. One image edit costs 5 credits and one text extraction costs 1 credit, and failed jobs are not charged. New accounts get 10 free credits to try the tools before buying anything.
What is the difference between the Monthly Plan and the Yearly Pass?
The Monthly Plan renews every month and its credits refresh each month; you can cancel anytime. The Yearly Pass is one payment for 12 months of credits, it does not renew automatically, and the credits stay valid for the full 12 months.
Are Yearly Pass credits added monthly or all at once?
All of them are added when you buy the pass, and they stay valid for 12 months.
Can I cancel my Monthly Plan?
Yes, anytime from your account settings. The plan stays active until the end of the month you have already paid for and does not renew after that. The Yearly Pass needs no cancellation because it never renews on its own.
What happens to unused credit points? Do paid credit points expire?
Plan credits have a life: Monthly Plan credits refresh each month and unused ones do not carry over, and Yearly Pass credits are valid for 12 months. The 10 free credits from sign-up have no expiry date.
What is the difference between paid credits and free credits?
New accounts get 10 free credits when they sign up. Paid plans add a fresh credit allowance every month or year, so heavy use does not eat into a one-off allowance.
How many images can I process?
Removing text or a watermark from one image costs 5 credits, and reading text out of an image costs 1 credit. So the 500-credit month covers about 100 removals, and the 6,000-credit Yearly Pass about 1,200.
What is your refund policy?
Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and run to the end of the month you paid for; we do not refund the unused part of a period you already started. If a job fails, its credits are returned to your account automatically. See our Terms of Use for the full policy.
What should I do if I paid but did not receive credits?
Contact support@pixpurge.com with your payment reference and we'll credit your account within 24 hours.
Why do some tools use more credits than others?
Cost depends on model, resolution, output quality, and processing complexity — heavier jobs consume more credits.
Can I use credits across all tools?
Yes — one credit balance works across every tool.
Do I get charged when I upload an image or video?
No — uploading is free; credits are only used when you process a job.
Why does the same tool show different credit costs?
Different modes and output options change the compute cost per job.
What happens if processing fails?
Failed jobs automatically refund the credits used for that job.
What should I do if credits were deducted incorrectly?
Contact support with the job details and we'll investigate and restore any wrong deductions.
Is my uploaded content private and secure?
Yes — files are processed in memory and never shared or used for training.
