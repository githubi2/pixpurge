Still Have Questions?

Find answers to the most common questions about credits, subscriptions, billing, and refunds.

What are credits and how do they work?
Credits are used to process tasks when certain features, modes, output quality, video length, or batch processing require credit usage. Different tools may consume different amounts of credits depending on the selected mode, AI model, resolution, file type, and processing needs. Daily free credits let you try all supported tools before purchasing.
What is the difference between One-Time Credits, Monthly Plan, and Annual Plan?
One-time credits are a single purchase that never expires. Monthly Plan renews each month; Annual Plan is billed once a year for the best per-credit rate.
Are Annual Plan credits issued monthly or all at once?
Annual Plan credits are issued monthly; unused credits roll over for the duration of your plan.
Can I cancel my Monthly Plan or Annual Plan?
Yes. You can cancel anytime from your account settings; the plan stays active until the end of the paid period.
What happens to unused credit points? Do paid credit points expire?
Paid credits do not expire; free daily credits reset every day.
What is the difference between paid credits and free credits?
Free credits are granted daily for trying the tools; paid credits are purchased packages with higher limits and priority usage.
How many times can I use each credit package?
Each package can be purchased once; you can stack packages by buying again when your balance runs low.
What is your refund policy?
Unused, unspent credit purchases are refundable within 14 days under our money-back guarantee.
What should I do if I paid but did not receive credits?
Contact support@pixpurge.com with your payment reference and we'll credit your account within 24 hours.
Why do some tools use more credits than others?
Cost depends on model, resolution, output quality, and processing complexity — heavier jobs consume more credits.
Can I use credits across all tools?
Yes — one credit balance works across every tool.
Do I get charged when I upload an image or video?
No — uploading is free; credits are only used when you process a job.
Why does the same tool show different credit costs?
Different modes and output options change the compute cost per job.
What happens if processing fails?
Failed jobs automatically refund the credits used for that job.
What should I do if credits were deducted incorrectly?
Contact support with the job details and we'll investigate and restore any wrong deductions.
Is my uploaded content private and secure?
Yes — files are processed in memory and never shared or used for training.
