PixPurge vs WatermarkRemover.io

Both remove marks automatically. One is built around stock watermarks and file formats; the other around text. Checked against their pricing and FAQ pages in November 2026.

Quick verdict

WatermarkRemover.io (built by PixelBin) is a watermark remover first: upload, it detects the mark, it fills it, done. Their free path is a two-a-day trial without an account and 3 credits after sign-up; batch work sits on the commercial plans, and the product advertises images, PDFs up to 25 pages and video up to two minutes.

PixPurge is text-first. A watermark is treated as a badge or a set of letters, which means the same button also clears captions, date stamps and screenshot labels — the things people usually end up removing by hand afterwards.

Neither is unconditionally better. For a stack of stock-style watermarks and a commercial plan, theirs is aimed exactly at that. For one photo where several kinds of text have to go at once, ours is the shorter route.

Short answer

  • WatermarkRemover.ioif you need PDF or video handling, batch work on a paid plan, or a tool built only around watermarks.
  • PixPurgeif the mark is text — a caption, a date stamp, a badge — or if several kinds of text have to go at once.
  • Bothassume the image is yours or licensed to you. Neither makes someone else's mark fair game.
