How It Works

Copy Text from an Image in Three Steps

No editing skills needed — here's the whole flow.

Step 1

Upload your image

Drag in a screenshot, a receipt photo, or a scanned page. JPG, PNG, WebP, and HEIC up to 10MB — you can also paste a screenshot straight in.

Step 2

Let the AI read it

PixPurge goes through the image and pulls out the words — printed text, screenshots, and clear handwriting alike. Most images take just a few seconds.

Step 3

Copy and paste it anywhere

Review the text, hit Copy Text, and drop it into your notes, your editor, or an email. It stays editable — nothing locked inside the picture.
