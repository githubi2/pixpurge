PixPurge vs Cleanup.pictures

Two ways to get text off a photo: one finds the text for you, the other waits for your brush. Here is how they actually differ, checked against both products in November 2026.

Quick verdict

If the problem is text, PixPurge is the shorter route. You upload the image, press one button, and every caption, timestamp and label it can see comes off — no tracing, no brush size to pick. The catch is that detection is not a menu: it takes all the text it finds, including the bits you liked.

Cleanup.pictures is a general inpainting tool. You paint over whatever should disappear — a watermark, a person, a cable, a scratch — and it fills the hole. That makes it wider in scope and slower for plain text: on a page with forty words, you brush forty words.

Neither wins outright. Pick by what is in the photo: text only, go PixPurge; text plus objects, go Cleanup.pictures; both, keep both open.
