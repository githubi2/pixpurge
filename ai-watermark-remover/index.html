AI Watermark Remover

Erase the visible badge from your own AI images — the Gemini sparkle, the ChatGPT mark, 'Generated with' strips. Clear about the line we do not cross.

Two different things

Visible Badge vs Hidden Provenance

"AI watermark" covers two unrelated mechanisms. One is a picture of a logo. The other is data you cannot see. We only deal with the first, and it matters that you know the difference.

Visible corner badge — yes

A sparkle in the corner, a small wordmark, "Generated with …" along one edge. It is painted into the pixels like any other graphic, so it can be erased like any other graphic.

What PixPurge does: finds the mark, removes the glyphs, rebuilds the corner from the surrounding image.

Hidden provenance — no

C2PA manifests sit in the file's metadata; SynthID and similar schemes embed an imperceptible signal in the pixels themselves. Neither is a badge, and neither is removed by editing the picture.

What PixPurge does: nothing, by design. Those signals survive pixel edits, and we do not claim otherwise.

The practical version: erasing the sparkle gives you a clean picture. It does not make the file indistinguishable from a camera photo, and it does not change what the file says about itself.

When removing the badge is reasonable

  • ✓It is your own generation and the badge clutters a product page, a print or a mockup
  • ✓You are placing the image inside a design where a corner logo breaks the layout
  • ✓The provider's terms allow it for your use — read them once, they differ per plan
  • ✓You are not presenting the result as a photograph or hiding that AI was used

When it is not

  • ×Evading a labelling rule — several jurisdictions now require AI images to be disclosed
  • ×Removing the mark from someone else's generation to pass it off as their work or yours
  • ×Using the cleaned image to imply a real event, a real person, or a camera capture
  • ×Assuming the edit wipes the provenance — it does not
